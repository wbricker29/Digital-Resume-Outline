## Overview
### Vision

Transform the professional networking and job search experience by replacing passive resume documents with an AI-powered conversational interface. Enable recruiters, hiring managers, and professional contacts to quickly assess qualifications through natural dialogue rather than document parsing—available 24/7 with accurate, contextual, and cited information.

### Core Value Proposition

**Problem:** Traditional resumes are passive documents that force readers to hunt for specific information. Recruiters waste time scanning PDFs, professionals struggle to keep profiles current, and candidates can't effectively differentiate themselves in competitive markets.

**Solution:** An intelligent, conversational interface that understands context, cites sources, and provides accurate answers about the candidate's background instantly. The system makes candidate information accessible through natural language queries while maintaining professional presentation.

### Project Goals

1. **Enable Efficient Discovery:** Allow visitors to find specific qualifications through conversational queries rather than document scanning
2. **Demonstrate Capabilities:** Showcase the candidate's technical, business, and AI expertise through both the content and the sophistication of the interaction
3. **Provide Always-Current Information:** Maintain a single source of truth that's easily updatable and always accessible
4. **Create Memorable Differentiation:** Stand out from traditional resumes and static portfolio sites through innovative presentation

### Target Users
Primary: Recruiters & Hiring Managers
Secondary: Professional Network
Tertiary: Potential Collaborators
